EX\_05NOV - Check Phone Battery Level

Read a phone's battery charge, a whole percent from 0 to 100.



Print three lines: whether the battery is low (below 20), whether it is fully charged (exactly 100), and how many percent are missing to a full charge. Each yes or no answer prints as 1 or 0.



e.g. 15

Low battery: 1

Fully charged: 0

Percent to full: 85
